## Si un día no estoy (original in Spanish)

Todo esto también es para que, si un día no estoy o llego a faltar, las personas que amo, quiero y aprecio tengan una manera bonita de recordarme y de sentirme cerca. Tengo la esperanza de que, si eso me llegara a pasar, alguno de mis amigos talentosos de la profesión, o la misma inteligencia artificial, me ayude a que este sitio y Roz perduren mucho más allá de lo que me lo permita el tiempo.

Espero vivir muchos años, y hasta llegar a ser abuelo. Pero prefiero ser responsable con quienes amo.

## If one day I am not here (English translation)

All of this is also so that, if one day I am not here, the people I love, care for and appreciate have a beautiful way to remember me and to feel me close. I hope that, if that ever happened, one of my talented friends of the profession, or artificial intelligence itself, will help this site and Roz last far beyond what time allows me.

I hope to live many years, and even to become a grandfather. But I would rather be responsible with the people I love.

## What this page is

[Roz](https://roz.montenegrodanielfelipe.com) is the assistant of this site: she answers questions about me from what this website publishes and from notes I wrote for her, over many hours of talking, so that she can answer a little as I would. Her code stays private. What she knows is mine, and it is below, exactly as she reads it, updated every time I teach her something new.

To give her back a voice, this page is enough, together with the pages of this site she reads (listed at [/assistant/index.json](/assistant/index.json)) and how she is designed, described on [her project page](/projects/roz/). **How Roz answers** is her voice and the answers I tuned one by one; the **core** is what she reads on every question; the **notes** are the details she looks up when a question needs them; **now** is what I was up to on the date it gives.
