**Visiones inestables** (*Unstable Visions*) is an artwork by the Colombian artist **[Mónica Páez](https://monicapaez.com/)**, made in collaboration with **Juan David García Arteaga** and **Daniel Felipe Montenegro**, and exhibited in 2026 at the **Universidad Nacional de Colombia**. It explores our interaction with **large language models** as a synthesized, amplified and distorted reflection of each person's digital footprint.

## 1. About the Artwork

The piece uses artificial intelligence to create **speculative versions of the possible futures of a well-known place**, starting from concrete material: **photographs of the Art Museum of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia** and **texts about the museum as an institution**.

The AI reorganizes and mixes discourses that have traditionally tried to stabilize ideas about **heritage, conservation, the archive, memory, authenticity** and the institution itself. Far from producing a coherent image of the museum, it generates **hybrid, ambiguous and contradictory images**, where the internal tensions of that material appear as errors, mutations and deviations.

The title refers to Harlan Ellison's anthology ***Dangerous Visions*** (1967), where science fiction is not a projection of the future but a tool to read the present. When the AI is asked to imagine possible futures for the museum, the images it returns reveal what usually remains invisible: its **mechanisms of conservation, its promises of permanence and its inevitable changes**.

> The instability is not in the eye that looks, but in the very nature of the institution.
>
> — <cite>From the artist's statement</cite>

## 2. Technique

- **Video projection**
- **Python 3 programming**
- **Text-guided image editing** with an agent based on the language model **gemini-2.5-flash-image**
- **Digital print** on Moab Lasal Photo matte 230 g paper
- Variable dimensions
